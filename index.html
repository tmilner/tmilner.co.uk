About me

Hello! I'm Tom, a Principal Software Engineer and Software Architect at Medidata, a Dassault Systèmes company, based in London. I build reliable systems for problems where getting the money and the data right really matters.

I lead engineering and architecture across four core teams in Clinical Trial Financial Management, working on payments, distributed systems, analytics and platform modernisation. I stay hands-on, so the technical direction I set is grounded in what it takes to build and run the systems.

I work closely with product and engineering leadership to understand the problems we're solving and shape how we deliver them. I enjoy sharing what I learn: I helped establish a Scala guild at Medidata, have spoken at London Scala meetups, and have delivered training across Medidata's R&D department.

Selected work

Across my roles at Medidata.

  • Patient and site payments

    I lead architecture and engineering for both product suites, coordinating delivery across teams and services and integrating third-party payment systems, so sites and patients get paid accurately and on time.

  • Event-driven pipelines

    Designed and built a resilient, self-healing pipeline that processes large batches from the data warehouse and streams the results through a multi-stage process. Each stage consumes events and emits new ones until the required results are generated, with strong data correctness guarantees throughout. Workers scale out to handle high throughput, and the pipeline recovers from failures without manual intervention.

  • Patient mobile integration

    Led integration with myMedidata, giving patients one place to manage payment preferences and tax information and to see what they'll be paid and when.

  • Reporting and modernisation

    Built a unified Snowflake reporting platform across financial management products. Led legacy product migrations onto the Medidata platform and broke monoliths into smaller, more scalable components.

  • Engineering practices and AI

    Helped streamline the software development lifecycle to deliver faster, and prototyped an early GraphQL API. I'm currently working on AI-based extraction of data from clinical sites' PDF invoices to cut down payment administration.
